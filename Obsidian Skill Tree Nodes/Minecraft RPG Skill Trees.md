## Try a build

1. Open a class planner. Set its playtest level, quest status and ability choices in the note properties.
2. In the Bases table, choose your subclass from the view menu. Click **Buy** to toggle an upgrade. Open a node name for its complete effect.
3. Read the **SP spent** total at the bottom. Your budget is **level − 1**, up to **49 SP**. Use the tier map and **Requires** column to review unlocks.
4. Uncheck purchases to refund them. Shared purchases are common to the two views for that class. Each subclass branch keeps its own draft purchases.

The planner lets you draft invalid combinations for comparison. Review the level, quest, tier and Ascension gates yourself. Passive and active selections are free and have one slot each.

### Smoke test

In Warrior → Duelist, buy **Endurance Training**, **Combat Footwork** and **Second Wind**. The table should show **4 SP spent**. This is valid at level 15 with **10 SP left**. At level 14, Second Wind fails its level gate. Uncheck Combat Footwork and Second Wind fails its two-node prerequisite even at level 15.
