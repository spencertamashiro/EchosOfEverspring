Everspring rose corner ornaments
================================
The four rose_*.png sources are original Echoes of Everspring artwork.
The upstream GUI-ASSET-NOTICE.txt identifies them as MIT-licensed and
generated from hand-authored pixel masks in tools/generate-container-themes.py.
They are scaled with nearest-neighbor interpolation in the skill atlas.
No vanilla container panels or Skill Tree (RPG Series) textures are copied.
