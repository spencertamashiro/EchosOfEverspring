GUI assets
==========
The rose and thorn corner ornaments are original artwork generated from the
hand-authored pixel masks in tools/generate-container-themes.py (project MIT license).

Container panel and GUI sprite variants are palette adaptations of the Minecraft
Java Edition 1.21.1 GUI artwork, copyright Mojang Studios / Microsoft. They preserve
the original layout and visual symbols for compatibility; the underlying Minecraft
artwork is not relicensed under this project's MIT license. Requires Minecraft.
